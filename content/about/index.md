Lu Wang (王璐) is a Professor in the College of Computer Science and Software Engineering at Shenzhen University. Her research covers wireless networks, the Internet of Things, mobile sensing, and human–AI interaction.

She received her Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) in 2014, advised by Prof. Mounir Hamdi, and her B.S. in Communication Engineering from Nankai University in 2009. She subsequently worked as a postdoctoral researcher with Prof. Qian Zhang at HKUST before joining Shenzhen University in June 2015.

## Research approach

Her work connects communication and sensing mechanisms with practical system design. Recent projects explore ambient LoRa communication, optical sensing for oral care and food inspection, assistive interfaces, and systems that help people inspect and refine AI outputs.

## Teaching

**Computer Networks** introduces network architecture, packet switching, Internet protocols, and networked applications. **Discrete Mathematics** develops foundations in logic, sets, algebraic structures, graph theory, and combinatorics. Both are undergraduate courses taught at Shenzhen University.

## Academic service

Lu Wang was appointed an Associate Editor of *IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing* in May 2023. Her service also includes TPC Chair for ICECI 2021, Workshop Chair for ICPADS 2017, and guest editorial work for *Peer-to-Peer Networking and Applications* and the *International Journal of Distributed Sensor Networks*.
