Gesture recognition and person identification are often implemented as separate radar models. MTxLSTM processes micro-Doppler and range-Doppler information in a multi-branch network, combining CNN features with xLSTM to share useful representations while retaining task-specific information.

The model uses gesture context to support identity recognition and individual motion characteristics to support gesture recognition. Reported experiments achieve 99.21% gesture accuracy and 98.59% person-identification accuracy. The contribution is a unified multi-task design that considers deployment complexity alongside recognition performance.
