A cancelled pickup can arise from a customer’s decision or from problems in courier behavior, and dialogue alone may not distinguish them. COCO+ combines customer–courier conversations with historical and current behavioral information. Hierarchical attention links the different information sources, while an LLM-assisted refinement stage makes use of unlabeled dialogue.

The extended framework adds customer behavior to improve identification of customer-related causes. Evaluation uses JD Logistics data and includes operational deployment and A/B testing. The contribution is a behavior-aware approach to interpreting cancellations in the context of how the service was actually carried out.
