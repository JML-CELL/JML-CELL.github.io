Millimeter-wave small cells have directional links that are sensitive to blockage and mobility. Management mechanisms developed for lower-frequency LTE networks cannot simply be transferred unchanged. This article examines how self-organizing networks can anticipate changing conditions instead of waiting for a performance problem before reacting.

The discussion connects planning, configuration, maintenance, optimization, and troubleshooting with the distinctive behavior of millimeter-wave links. Its contribution is a proactive management perspective and a set of research issues for making dense small-cell deployments more autonomous.
