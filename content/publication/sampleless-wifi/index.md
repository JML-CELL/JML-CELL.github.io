The journal study develops standard-compatible reception at reduced sampling rates. Rather than asking the transmitter to send a special packet, the receiver combines redundancy from retransmissions and uses time-shift-induced constellation diversity to recover information missing from any single undersampled observation.

Implementation and trace-based evaluation examine the relationship between decoding capability and energy use. The contribution is a receiver-centered approach to low-power Wi-Fi, making existing retransmission behavior useful for devices whose workload does not justify full-rate sampling.
