The system measures the floor impact and temporal vibration pattern associated with a fall using geophones. A Hidden Markov Model represents the sequence of a falling event, allowing a template learned in advance to be applied without collecting fall-training examples from each elderly user. An Energy-of-Arrival positioning stage helps reconfirm candidate events.

The study examines recognition and false alarms rather than relying on impact strength alone. Its contribution is a device-free monitoring pipeline that combines temporal event modeling with spatial confirmation, reducing the need for wearable equipment or individual training sessions.
