Ambient backscatter receivers must recover weak reflected data from signals originally intended for other wireless users. This article explains that detection challenge and reviews machine-learning-assisted solutions, comparing how they use data to deal with the unknown or changing properties of the ambient signal.

The discussion connects detection performance with the operating principles of backscatter networks and outlines future research directions. Its contribution is a structured review of where learning can help low-power receivers, and which communication-specific difficulties remain.
