SIDE treats UAV placement as a semi-distributed adjustment process. EMech models users’ locations and traffic demands as attractions that move an aerial base station toward a mechanical equilibrium. KDivision partitions the service area according to user density, and RMerge combines areas to balance service quality and deployment cost.

Field experiments test the feasibility of the movement mechanism, while simulations evaluate the wider deployment strategy. The contribution is a connection between local user demand and network-level UAV placement, reducing dependence on a fully centralized static layout.
