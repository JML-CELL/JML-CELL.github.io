Machine-type devices often need deep coverage, infrequent small transmissions, low cost, and long battery life rather than high throughput. The survey explains how NB-IoT builds on the LTE ecosystem to address these requirements and how deployment choices affect coexistence with existing cellular infrastructure.

It reviews performance analysis, design optimization, integration with other technologies, implementation, and applications. The contribution is a broad technical map of NB-IoT’s development and remaining problems, connecting radio mechanisms with the practical requirements of large-scale IoT deployments.
