In distributed cognitive radio networks, nodes must agree on available spectrum and then decide who can transmit. This extended FCM study designs both steps for OFDM systems, using frequency-domain coordination to reduce the cost of cooperation and contention without abandoning their sensing and access functions.

Simulation experiments examine control overhead and network throughput. The work makes explicit the connection between reliable spectrum discovery and efficient medium access, providing a unified protocol view of two stages that compete for the same radio resources.
