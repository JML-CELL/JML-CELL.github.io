Rateless coding adapts transmission to channel quality, but a multicarrier link can have very different conditions on individual subcarriers. Full Duplex Rateless coding uses fine-grained feedback in the frequency domain to exploit this diversity, seeking a suitable rate for each subcarrier rather than relying on one coarse link-wide choice.

Simulations compare the approach with fixed-rate LDPC and Spinal codes. The contribution is to use feedback as a means of exposing frequency diversity to the code, connecting rate adaptation with the structure of a multicarrier channel.
