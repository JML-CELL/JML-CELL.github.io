Wider Wi-Fi channels increase the likelihood of partial overlap. Instead of treating every overlap as an unusable collision, InterRandom studies how interference affects symbols at the physical layer. It randomizes interference according to the overlapping portion so that coding redundancy can be used more effectively during concurrent transmissions.

This shifts the design question from simply avoiding adjacent channels to managing the damage they introduce. The paper connects channel overlap, interference patterns, and packet recovery, offering a physical-layer basis for more flexible spectrum sharing in dense Wi-Fi networks.
