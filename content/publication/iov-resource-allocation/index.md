Vehicle networks must allocate radio resources while traffic demand and channel conditions change. The proposed framework treats base stations in a cluster as collaborating agents and uses Proximal Policy Optimization to coordinate transmission power and bandwidth. This makes resource allocation a joint decision across cells rather than a set of independent local choices.

Simulation experiments compare the method with reinforcement-learning alternatives including SAC and A2C. The evaluation considers throughput, power efficiency, and bandwidth allocation. The contribution is a collaborative learning formulation for managing the competing communication requirements of clustered Internet-of-Vehicles networks.
