Directional millimeter-wave beams reduce interference, but a limited number of beams still constrains how many UAV-network users can transmit concurrently. The article studies constellation-division multiple access, using flexible constellation division and rateless coding to add multiplexing opportunities within a beam-based system.

It connects the multiple-access design with beamwidth adaptation and interference management in mobile aerial networks. The contribution is a system-level view of how coding, signal constellations, and directional antennas can work together when conventional beam-division access reaches its limits.
