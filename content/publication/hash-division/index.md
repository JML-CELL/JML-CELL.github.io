Hash Division Multiple Access uses redundancy in dense signal constellations as an additional dimension for separating users. An orthogonal, linear, random encoder constructs a hash space at the physical layer, while an access-point-driven MAC coordinates uplink and downlink transmissions that share the medium.

The design is implemented on GNU Radio and assessed through trace-driven emulation. Its contribution is a connection between constellation design and multiple access, studying how controlled symbol distances and coding can support concurrent users rather than only increasing a single link’s rate.
