Wireless coordination consumes airtime that could otherwise carry application data. Attachment transmission addresses this cost by sending control information together with an ongoing data signal. The book develops the physical-layer idea and its use in coordinating access among wireless stations.

As a research monograph, it connects the underlying communication model with protocol-level applications. It provides a unified account of attachment transmission for readers interested in how control signaling and useful data can share wireless resources more efficiently.
