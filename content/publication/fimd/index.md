Indoor motion changes Wi-Fi channel state information, but raw received signal strength is easily affected by multipath. FIMD extracts features from the temporal stability and frequency diversity of CSI. A density-based DBSCAN detector identifies departures from the static environment, with false-alert filtering and data fusion improving reliability.

The prototype uses commercial IEEE 802.11n network cards and is evaluated in two indoor environments. The study demonstrates device-free motion detection from commodity wireless measurements and examines why fine-grained channel information can be more robust than signal strength alone.
