Edge applications share communication links and computing capacity, so improving one resource in isolation may leave another as the bottleneck. The proposed software-defined architecture exposes finer-grained control of networking and computation, allowing the two to be managed together for heterogeneous devices and delay-sensitive services.

A two-stage Q-learning resource-allocation strategy adapts to radio conditions and user requirements. Simulation experiments examine the architecture’s ability to support computation offloading and service delivery. The work connects cloud–network integration with coordinated resource decisions at the mobile edge.
