CLOT begins with a switchless torus and adds selected low-end switches to shorten otherwise long communication paths. The torus preserves locality between nearby servers, while the additional switch layer improves communication across the data center. Coordinate-based addressing and the POW routing algorithm support forwarding over this structure.

The design includes credit-based flow control, address configuration, and failure detection. Analysis examines latency, routing distance, bisection bandwidth, throughput, and fault tolerance. The contribution is a network architecture that balances local connectivity, global routing efficiency, and infrastructure cost.
