Highly mobile vehicles experience rapid changes across both time and frequency, making a single coarse video-delivery rate unreliable. iCast combines source and channel protection through a joint rateless-coding design, adapting delivery to subcarrier-level conditions while accounting for the protection required by the video stream.

Experiments compare video quality with competing wireless delivery schemes. The contribution is a fine-grained link between the source data and wireless channel, using frequency diversity to maintain useful video delivery in mobile vehicular environments.
