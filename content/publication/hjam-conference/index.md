A conventional WLAN alternates control exchanges with data traffic, so coordination consumes a share of the available channel time. HJam explores attachment transmission: control information accompanies a data transmission, allowing coordination and useful traffic to overlap rather than always taking separate turns.

This INFOCOM paper introduces the communication idea at the conference stage. The later [journal extension](/publication/hjam-journal/) develops the model and evaluation in greater detail. Keeping the two records separate distinguishes the original contribution from its expanded treatment.
