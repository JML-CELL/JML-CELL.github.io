Backscatter tags communicate by modulating reflected signals, creating opportunities for sensing with very small energy budgets. This survey examines sensing based on radio, acoustic, and visible-light backscatter and discusses applications including physiological monitoring, orientation estimation, and localization.

The review organizes performance questions around power management, channel models, coverage, throughput, signal quality, modulation, coding, and security. Its contribution is a sensing-centered view of backscatter technology that connects communication mechanisms to deployment requirements and identifies open problems for future low-power sensing systems.
