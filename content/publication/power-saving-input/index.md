This extended Taprint study uses hand-tapping vibrations for both PIN entry and user authentication. It reuses a smartwatch’s inertial sensor and applies cubic-spline interpolation and feature refinement to recover useful detail from the available samples. The same tap therefore carries information about location and the person producing it.

Experiments with 128 users examine recognition, authentication, and everyday disturbances. The reported keystroke-recognition accuracy is 96%, with a 2.2% single-tap authentication error rate. The work connects wearable input, security, and the constraints of commodity sensing hardware.
