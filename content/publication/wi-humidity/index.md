Humidity affects radio propagation, but received signal strength also changes with multipath and other spatial or temporal effects. WiHumidity uses the diversity of channel state information to reduce this ambiguity and extract information associated with environmental humidity from commodity Wi-Fi measurements.

Experiments evaluate the feasibility of humidity measurement using this richer channel representation. The contribution is to investigate an environmental-sensing use of existing wireless signals, with particular attention to why a single received-power reading is insufficient in an indoor setting.
