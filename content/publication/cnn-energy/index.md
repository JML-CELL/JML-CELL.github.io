On-device inference reduces response latency, but repeated arithmetic and parameter reads can quickly drain a mobile device’s battery. This survey separates energy-saving approaches into three families: compressing the neural network, reducing data movement during computation, and offloading work to other computing resources.

It also compares three model-compression techniques on an object-recognition task. The contribution is a practical organization of the design space, helping relate a sensing application’s accuracy, latency, memory traffic, and energy requirements to the appropriate optimization strategy.
