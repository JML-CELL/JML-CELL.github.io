Long-tailed data contains many examples of common classes but few of rare ones; in IoT deployments, these imbalances may also differ across participants. FELT studies federated ensemble learning in this setting, combining the knowledge of multiple local models through communication-efficient private voting.

The research brings together three practical concerns: class imbalance, the cost of exchanging information, and privacy during collaboration. Its focus is how distributed models can contribute to a shared prediction process when the data available at individual devices is uneven and communication is constrained.
