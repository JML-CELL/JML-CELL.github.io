Water vapor affects millimeter-wave propagation, but humidity is not the only environmental influence on the received signal. The method uses subspace projection to suppress unrelated effects, extracts humidity-associated features, and trains a support vector machine to distinguish humidity levels in a particular environment.

Experiments examine sensitivity at different humidity intervals and in different scenarios. The contribution is a signal-processing pipeline that separates the desired environmental property from other propagation effects, making radio measurements more useful for fine-grained humidity sensing.
