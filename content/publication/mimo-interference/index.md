MIMO can improve WLAN capacity, but the benefit depends on how interference is handled across links. The survey introduces the evolution of IEEE 802.11 and the relevant physical- and MAC-layer mechanisms, then separates interference management in single-collision-domain and multiple-collision-domain settings.

This organization makes it possible to compare how spatial processing and access coordination interact at different network scales. The paper synthesizes existing mechanisms, discusses their limitations, and identifies research challenges for improving the capacity of MIMO-based wireless LANs.
