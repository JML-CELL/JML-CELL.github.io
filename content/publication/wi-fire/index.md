Fire changes the propagation of radio signals, producing changes in the amplitude and phase of Wi-Fi CSI. Wi-Fire investigates whether these effects can distinguish a fire event using existing wireless infrastructure. The prototype runs on desktop computers with commercial IEEE 802.11n network cards.

Experiments in different environments assess the detectability of the signal changes. The research contributes an exploratory device-free sensing method based on channel measurements, connecting the physical effect of fire on radio propagation with a practical prototype using existing wireless hardware.
