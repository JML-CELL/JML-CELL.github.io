ViType uses a single compact vibration sensor to recognize typing on the body at a lower sampling rate than multi-sensor alternatives. A neural recognition model extracts information from limited vibration measurements, while runtime calibration and adaptation handle changes that would otherwise reduce accuracy over time.

Experiments with 30 participants examine recognition and robustness under different conditions. The conference paper reports 94.8% average initial recognition accuracy and investigates improvement through continued calibration. Its contribution is a portable on-body input design with modest sensing requirements.
