Taprint places a virtual numeric keypad on the back of the hand and uses the wristband’s inertial sensor to detect taps. The vibration of hand landmarks carries both location information and user-specific characteristics, so the design combines PIN entry with biometric authentication instead of treating them as separate interactions.

Experiments with 128 users assess keystroke recognition, authentication, and practical disturbances. The conference study reports 96% recognition accuracy and a 2.4% single-tap authentication error rate. Its contribution is a compact input-and-security mechanism built around commodity wristband sensing.
