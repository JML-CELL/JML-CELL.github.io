Microbial growth changes both the surface tension and the absorption characteristics of a drink, often before deterioration becomes obvious. Microbe-Radar measures these changes with eight LEDs and a photodiode. Characteristic-offset measurement and spectral-dimension expansion extract complementary information, while additional processing suppresses ambient light and liquid sloshing.

The study evaluates measurements of surface tension and absorption spectra, then investigates contamination detection and deterioration warnings across beverages. The contribution is a lightweight optical monitoring system that connects physical changes in a liquid with quality-related indicators during storage.
