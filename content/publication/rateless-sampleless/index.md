High Wi-Fi sampling rates can waste energy on devices that carry little traffic. Inspired by rateless coding, Sampleless Wi-Fi accumulates information across retransmissions to recover packets received at a lower sampling rate. Receiver-side time shifts create constellation diversity without changing the standard packet format.

GNU Radio/USRP experiments and real Wi-Fi traces evaluate decoding and energy efficiency against downclocking approaches. The conference paper demonstrates how retransmission redundancy can be turned into a resource for energy-constrained receivers while preserving compatibility with existing packets.
