A fixed access-point layout can localize some areas well and others poorly. NomLoc uses movable access points to alter the network geometry and reduce this spatial performance imbalance. A space-partitioning method performs calibration-free positioning, while fine-grained CSI helps reduce errors caused by multipath and non-line-of-sight propagation.

The system is implemented using off-the-shelf devices and evaluated in two indoor environments, including errors in the reported positions of the movable access points. The work contributes an active infrastructure design for improving localization consistency across a space.
