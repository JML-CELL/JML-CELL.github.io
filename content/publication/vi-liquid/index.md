Vi-liquid turns a smartphone’s vibration motor and motion sensor into a liquid-measurement system. A viscosity-based model describes how different liquids respond to active vibration. Signal-processing stages reconstruct undersampled measurements and suppress self-interference and volume-related effects.

The conference study evaluates 30 liquids, reporting 95.47% average identification accuracy and a 2.9% mean relative viscosity-estimation error. The contribution is a physically grounded approach to liquid identification with commodity mobile hardware rather than a specialized laboratory instrument.
