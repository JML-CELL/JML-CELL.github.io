Video streams and other wireless traffic have different service requirements. A time-domain hybrid MAC can serve only one protocol style at a time. FC-MAC instead partitions the frequency band into fine-grained subchannels and combines access protocols across frequency, adapting bandwidth and protocol choices to user demand and channel conditions.

Simulation experiments compare the design with time-domain hybrid access. The contribution is a frequency-domain approach to heterogeneous traffic: it seeks to preserve video quality of experience while allowing different types of traffic to share the channel concurrently.
