AI-assisted contract review can save effort, but uncertain suggestions create a risk of either excessive reliance or unnecessary distrust. A contextual investigation with 24 attorneys examines where commercial tools conflict with real review practices. The findings inform seven interaction components, developed with attorneys and assembled into a Wizard-of-Oz prototype.

A subsequent study with 16 attorneys compares perceived trustworthiness with the system’s capabilities. The work examines how evidence and interaction support informed review decisions and reports improved trust calibration relative to commercial tools. Its main contribution is a scenario-grounded design approach for helping professionals judge AI assistance.
