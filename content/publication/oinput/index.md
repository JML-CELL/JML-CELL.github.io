Small wearable screens offer little room for a keyboard, while acoustic and motion-based input can be sensitive to surroundings. Oinput models vibrations transmitted through human bones and uses them to recognize a QWERTY-style input layout. A neural recognition model and calibration mechanism address noise and changing input conditions.

Experiments with 30 volunteers evaluate recognition and robustness. The design also considers layout, feature selection, and computational cost to reduce energy demand. Its contribution is an anatomy-informed input channel that extends a wearable device’s interaction area beyond its screen.
