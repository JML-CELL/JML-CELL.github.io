Obstacles and multipath make radio-based localization difficult in complex rooms. FLoc takes a different sensing route: geophones measure floor vibrations produced by footsteps. Its pipeline detects footsteps, suppresses environmental interference, and uses the recovered seismic signals to estimate the walker’s location without attaching a device to the person.

The prototype uses Raspberry Pi computers connected to geophones. Experiments in a 6 × 8 m indoor area examine different conditions and report average localization accuracy as fine as 7 cm in the tested setting. The study demonstrates the value of directional floor-vibration information for indoor positioning.
