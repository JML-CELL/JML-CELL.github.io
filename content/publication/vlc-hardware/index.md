The speed of a visible-light link depends on the response of its emitters, color converters, and photodetectors. This review compares hardware using modulation bandwidth, response time, and data-rate measurements, explaining how material and device limits constrain the communication channel.

It also discusses trade-offs such as the relationship between detector area, bandwidth, and field of view, including fluorescent-antenna approaches. The contribution is an overview that connects advances in individual optical components with the requirements of complete visible-light communication systems.
