Many fitness trackers record movement without measuring the neural and muscular activity associated with an exercise. Muscle-Mind explores this gap using a wearable EEG hairband and an EMG shirt with non-invasive surface electrodes. The poster investigates the relationship between mental effort and muscle activation during upper-limb free-weight exercises.

As an exploratory poster, the contribution is a combined biosignal-sensing concept and an initial exercise study. It broadens strength-training feedback beyond motion alone, while leaving the scope of its evidence tied to the reported prototype and participants.
