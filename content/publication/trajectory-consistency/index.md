Trajectory prediction depends on the observations available before the prediction point. When detections or tracks are incomplete, filling in the missing observations and predicting the future become related tasks. This work studies them collaboratively, using consistency evaluation to connect reconstructed observations with predicted movement.

The research focuses on how the two tasks can inform one another rather than assuming an entirely complete input sequence. It brings observation quality into the prediction problem itself, addressing a practical source of uncertainty in systems that reason about movement from imperfect sensing data.
