During storage, microbial activity can alter a liquor’s surface tension and absorption spectrum before visible changes become clear. Lili measures both with eight LEDs and a photodiode. Its processing expands the available spectral information and extracts characteristic offsets, while compensating for ambient light and sloshing.

The evaluation connects optical measurements with contamination duration, microbial content, and composition. The contribution is a lightweight monitoring approach for stored liquids that uses complementary physical indicators instead of relying solely on slow culture-based sampling.
