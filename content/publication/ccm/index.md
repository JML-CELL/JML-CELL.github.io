CCM studies communication between underwater-vehicle swarms and mobile aerial nodes. It combines acoustic and optical communication with UAV mobility, using multiple aerial vehicles as mobile data-collection points. A cooperative movement strategy uses multi-agent reinforcement learning to adapt coverage as underwater vehicles move.

The design also addresses optical alignment through adaptive pointing and beam-divergence control. Simulations use underwater-vehicle trajectories and ocean-current data to examine behavior in changing maritime conditions. The contribution is a joint treatment of mobility, coverage, and cross-boundary link management.
