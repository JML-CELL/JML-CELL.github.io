The iKey demonstration extends a wristband’s input area onto the back of the user’s hand. Tapping produces body vibrations that can be associated with locations on a virtual keyboard, using a location-based training model to recognize the intended key.

The paper presents a working real-time prototype. As a demonstration contribution, its emphasis is the interaction concept and implementation: showing that a surface beyond a tiny touchscreen can become an input interface for a wearable device.
