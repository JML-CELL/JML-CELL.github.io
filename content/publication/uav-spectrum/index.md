Dense UAV deployments can provide flexible coverage but also create strong interference between nearby cells. SpecShare investigates overlapped spectrum sharing by exploiting coding redundancy at the physical layer. The article examines the architecture and signal-processing principles that make partially concurrent transmissions useful.

The discussion also considers UAV placement, because network geometry affects the gain available from spectrum sharing. The contribution is a joint view of deployment and physical-layer design for improving throughput in dense, on-demand aerial networks.
