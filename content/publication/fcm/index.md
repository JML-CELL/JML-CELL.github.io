Cooperative spectrum sensing improves detection by combining observations from several radios, but sharing decisions and competing for channels can consume the resulting gain. FCM moves these coordination operations into the frequency domain, allowing sensing cooperation and multichannel contention to be represented with less time-domain signaling.

The paper evaluates this MAC design in cognitive radio ad hoc networks through simulation. Its contribution is to treat sensing overhead and access overhead together, so that identifying unused spectrum also leads to efficient use of that spectrum.
