X.509 certificates underpin much of the Internet’s authentication infrastructure, but issuing, validating, and revoking certificates creates several different security and operational challenges. The survey reviews the public-key infrastructure and the ways attacks can exploit weaknesses in its trust and certificate-management processes.

It also examines modern approaches involving blockchain and ledger technologies, relating them to established certificate and revocation mechanisms. The contribution is a structured view of the security problem and the implementation landscape, helping distinguish changes in trust management from changes in how certificate status is recorded and distributed.
