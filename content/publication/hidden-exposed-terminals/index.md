This journal study develops FAST as a combined solution to hidden and exposed terminals. Physical-layer Attachment Coding supplies channel-usage information without replacing the data transmission, while MAC-layer Attachment Sense uses that information to detect conflicts and identify otherwise missed concurrent transmissions.

The paper includes a feasibility analysis, an eight-node USRP2/GNU Radio implementation, and network simulations. It shows how richer local knowledge can improve access decisions while keeping the cost of exchanging control information central to the design.
