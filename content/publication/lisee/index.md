Finding and reaching a nearby object involves more than recognizing its category: the user also needs usable guidance about where to move. LiSee integrates binocular cameras, a microphone, and audio feedback into a neckband-headphone form. Users request an object through speech, and the system combines object perception with personalized guidance during reaching.

The image-processing pipeline addresses motion and changing viewpoints caused by the wearable form factor. System tests and user studies in the laboratory and participants’ homes examine robustness and practical use. The contribution is an integrated assistive interaction that brings object search and reaching guidance into an everyday wearable.
