Hidden terminals cause collisions, while exposed terminals unnecessarily defer transmissions. FAST addresses their shared information problem: neighboring nodes need to know who is sending and who is receiving. Attachment Coding conveys that information at the physical layer, and Attachment Sense uses it to distinguish the two situations at the MAC layer.

The system is evaluated through simulations of ad hoc networks. Its central contribution is a shared channel-usage mechanism for two problems that conventional carrier sensing often handles separately, making access decisions more informative than a single busy-or-idle indicator.
