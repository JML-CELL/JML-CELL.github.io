LiSee 是面向盲人和低视力用户的辅助耳机系统，将物体感知与语音引导结合，帮助用户定位并伸手获取周围物品。

**LiSee: A Headphone that Provides All-day Assistance for Blind and Low-vision Users to Reach Surrounding Objects** 于 2022 年发表于 *Proceedings of the ACM on Interactive, Mobile, Wearable and Ubiquitous Technologies*。
