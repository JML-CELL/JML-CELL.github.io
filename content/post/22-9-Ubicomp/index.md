LiSee is an assistive headphone system for blind and low-vision users. It combines object perception with spoken guidance to help users locate and reach objects around them.

**LiSee: A Headphone that Provides All-day Assistance for Blind and Low-vision Users to Reach Surrounding Objects** was published in *Proceedings of the ACM on Interactive, Mobile, Wearable and Ubiquitous Technologies* (2022).
