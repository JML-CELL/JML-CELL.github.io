Chameleon separates and identifies overlapping keystroke signals. Its adaptive design addresses simultaneous inputs that are difficult to recognize using models built for isolated keystrokes.

**Chameleon: An Adaptive System for Overlapping Keystroke Signal Separation and Identification** was published in *2024 IEEE 30th International Conference on Parallel and Distributed Systems (ICPADS)* (2024).
