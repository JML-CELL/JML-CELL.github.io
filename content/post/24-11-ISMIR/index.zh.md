Chameleon 分离并识别相互叠加的击键信号，通过自适应设计处理同时输入，解决基于单次独立击键建立的模型难以识别重叠输入的问题。

**Chameleon: An Adaptive System for Overlapping Keystroke Signal Separation and Identification** 于 2024 年发表于 *2024 IEEE 30th International Conference on Parallel and Distributed Systems (ICPADS)*。
