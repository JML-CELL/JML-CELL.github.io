PIT 在刷牙过程中提供实时牙菌斑指示，通过光学感知帮助用户定位染色后的牙菌斑，即使牙膏泡沫遮挡牙齿表面也能提供反馈。

**PIT: A Novel Toothbrush Providing Real-Time and Robust Plaque Indication During Brushing** 于 2025 年发表于 *Proceedings of the 23rd Annual International Conference on Mobile Systems, Applications and Services*。
