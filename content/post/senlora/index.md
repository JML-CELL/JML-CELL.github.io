SenLoRa integrates sensing with ambient LoRa communication. It reuses information in ordinary network traffic to enable sensing while retaining standard communication capabilities.

**SenLoRa: Integrated Sensing and Communication with Ambient LoRa** was published in *Proceedings of the ACM on Interactive, Mobile, Wearable and Ubiquitous Technologies* (2025).
