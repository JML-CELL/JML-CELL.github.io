Beyond Vision 将植物电信号与同步视频相结合，用于害虫识别。研究提出 HIPB-MM 数据集和 HIPS 框架，探索视觉线索之外的害虫取食活动感知。

**Beyond Vision: A Multimodal Dataset and Framework for Pest Recognition via Plant Electrophysiological Signals** 于 2026 年发表于 *Proceedings of the Thirty-Fifth International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence*。
