Beyond Vision combines plant electrical signals with synchronized video for pest recognition. It introduces the HIPB-MM dataset and the HIPS framework to study feeding activity beyond visual cues alone.

**Beyond Vision: A Multimodal Dataset and Framework for Pest Recognition via Plant Electrophysiological Signals** was published in *Proceedings of the Thirty-Fifth International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence* (2026).
